## Screen recordings, ready to share

WebM is the video format of the web. Browser-based screen recorders, many
capture tools, and some video sites save to WebM. That makes it the format you
often have when you want to show a bug, a product feature, or a quick how-to.

A GIF of that recording plays on its own and loops in places video can't:
pull requests, issue trackers, help docs, email, and chat threads.

## Built for quick demos

- **Trim to the action.** Drag the start and end points to cut the setup and
  the pause at the end. A GIF can run up to 30 seconds.
- **Crop to what matters.** Use 1:1, 16:9, or 9:16 to frame one part of the
  screen.
- **Speed it up.** A faster playback speed squeezes a slow demo into a few
  seconds.
- **Add a label.** A short caption can explain what the viewer is looking at.

## Private by default

Screen recordings often show things you'd rather not upload: dashboards,
emails, or code. Snipgif converts the WebM on your device with ffmpeg.wasm, so
the recording never leaves your computer.

## Getting a sharp, small GIF

Screen content has crisp text and flat colors, so it compresses well as a GIF.
Keep the width close to the size the GIF will be shown at, and use a lower
frame rate for slow-moving demos. If the file is still too big for where you
want to post it, the GIF Compressor can reduce it further.
