## Turn any MP4 clip into a GIF

MP4 is the format most phones, cameras, and screen recorders save to, so it is
usually the video you already have. Snipgif turns it into a GIF that plays
everywhere: in chat apps, email, documents, and on websites that don't play
video.

The whole conversion happens in your browser. Your MP4 is never sent to a
server, so private clips stay private, and you don't wait for an upload to
finish before you can start.

## Pick the exact moment

Most videos are longer than the GIF you want. The timeline lets you drag a
start and end point and watch the preview until the clip is just right. GIFs
can run up to 30 seconds, but shorter clips loop better and stay small.

## Make it fit where it's going

- **Crop** to 1:1 for profile posts, 16:9 for wide screens, or 9:16 for
  stories.
- **Change the speed**, play it in reverse, or turn it into a back-and-forth
  boomerang loop.
- **Add a caption** in meme style, with your choice of font, color, and
  position.
- **Pick a preset**: "Small for chat" keeps files light, "Balanced" suits most
  uses, and "High quality" keeps more detail. You can also set the frame rate
  and width yourself.

## Keep the file small

GIFs store every frame as a full image, so they grow much larger than the MP4
they came from. Shorter clips, a lower frame rate, and a smaller width make the
biggest difference. If a finished GIF is still too large, run it through the
GIF Compressor to cut colors or skip frames.
