## MOV to GIF, without the upload

MOV is the video format Apple devices use. Every clip you record on an iPhone or
iPad, and every screen recording you make with QuickTime on a Mac, is usually a
MOV file. Snipgif turns these clips into GIFs you can send to anyone, whatever
phone or computer they use.

Your video is converted on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, so family
videos and work recordings stay with you.

## Videos your browser can't play still convert

Newer iPhones often record in HEVC, which some browsers can't play. When that
happens, Snipgif shows still-frame previews of the clip instead of the moving
video. You can still trim, crop, and convert as usual, and the GIF comes out the
same.

## Trim, crop, and caption

Phone videos tend to be long, and the moment you want is a few seconds of it.
Drag the start and end points on the timeline to pick your clip, up to 30
seconds long.

Vertical phone videos look best cropped to 9:16 for stories or 1:1 for posts.
Add a caption with your own text, font, and color, or change the speed for a
slow-motion or sped-up effect.

## Tips for iPhone users

- **Pick a short moment.** A 3 to 5 second clip makes a GIF that loads quickly
  in messages.
- **Use a smaller width.** Phone videos are high resolution, but chat apps show
  GIFs small. The "Small for chat" preset handles this for you.
- **Long videos use a lot of memory.** If your phone runs out of memory, choose
  a shorter clip or a smaller width and try again.
