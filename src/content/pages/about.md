Snipgif is a free online tool that turns short videos into GIFs. Drop in a
video, pick the moment you want, adjust the size and speed, and download a GIF
in under a minute. There is also a [GIF compressor](/gif-compressor) for
shrinking GIFs you already have.

## Your files stay on your device

Most online converters upload your video to a server, process it there, and
send the result back. Snipgif works differently. It runs the conversion inside
your browser with [ffmpeg.wasm](https://ffmpegwasm.netlify.app/), a version of
the FFmpeg video toolkit built for the web. Your videos and GIFs are never
uploaded, never stored by Snipgif, and are gone when you close the tab.

## Free, with no catches

- **No watermark.** Snipgif never adds a logo or mark to your GIFs.
- **No sign-up.** There are no accounts. Open the page and start.
- **Free to use.** Snipgif is supported by ads, which never block the converter.

## Who it is for

- People sharing funny moments and memes on WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram, X, and
  Reddit.
- Creators and marketers adding motion to social posts, newsletters, and email
  campaigns, where GIFs play but videos often do not.
- Developers and technical writers turning screen recordings into GIFs for
  READMEs, bug reports, and docs.
- Students and teachers making short animations for presentations and lessons.
