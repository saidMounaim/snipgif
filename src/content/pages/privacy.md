This policy explains what information Snipgif collects when you use
snipgif.com, why, and the choices you have. Snipgif is the operator of this
website and is responsible for the personal data described here.

## Your videos and GIFs never leave your device

Snipgif converts videos and compresses GIFs inside your browser. The files you
choose are read and processed on your device only. They are never uploaded to
Snipgif or anyone else, Snipgif never sees or stores them, and they are cleared
from memory when you close or reload the page.

## Information processed when you visit

### Hosting

The site is hosted on [Cloudflare Pages](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
Like any web server, Cloudflare receives technical details with every request,
such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested. These are used to
deliver the site, keep it secure, and protect it from abuse.

### The converter engine

When you choose a video to convert or a GIF to compress, your browser downloads
the converter engine (about 30 MB) from the
[jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net) content
delivery network. jsDelivr receives your IP address and browser details as part
of that download. Your files are not sent.

### The contact form

If you send a message through the [contact page](/contact), the form sends your
email address, your name if you give one, and your message to
[Formspree](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy), which forwards it to
Snipgif by email. If you email Snipgif directly, the same information arrives
through your email provider. Snipgif uses it only to reply to you and keeps it
only as long as needed to handle your request.

### Information stored on your device

When you use the theme switch, Snipgif saves your choice in your browser's
local storage under the key `theme`. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. See
the [Cookie Policy](/cookies) for details.

## Advertising and analytics from Google

Snipgif is free because it shows ads. Snipgif uses, or will use, the following
Google services to show ads and understand how the site is used:

- **Google AdSense** shows ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and
  similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other
  websites, to limit how often you see an ad, and to measure ad performance.
- **Google Analytics 4** measures visits and how the tools are used, such as
  pages viewed, conversions completed, and settings chosen. It does not receive
  your videos or GIFs.
- **Google's consent management platform** asks visitors in the European
  Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland for consent before
  personalized ads or analytics cookies are used, and stores that choice.

To learn more, read
[how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
You can turn off personalized ads in
[Google's My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/) and opt out of
Google Analytics with the
[Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## How long data is kept

Snipgif has no server or database of its own, so it keeps no records of your
visits. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your request,
as described above. Cloudflare, jsDelivr, Formspree, and Google keep the data
they receive under their own privacy policies, linked above.

## Legal bases

For visitors in the European Union and the United Kingdom, Snipgif relies on:

- **Legitimate interests** for hosting, security, and delivering the converter
  engine, which are needed to run the site.
- **Consent** for personalized advertising and analytics cookies, where the law
  requires it. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legitimate interests** for answering messages you choose to send.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or
delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data
portability, and to withdraw consent. Snipgif does not hold an account or
profile about you, so most data described here is held by the services named
above, and you can also contact them directly.

Snipgif processes personal data in line with Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the
protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, and
with the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for visitors from
those regions. You can file a complaint with Morocco's CNDP (Commission
Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel) or
with the data protection authority where you live.

To make a request, email Snipgif at the address below. Snipgif will reply
within 30 days.

## Children

Snipgif is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect
personal data from them.
