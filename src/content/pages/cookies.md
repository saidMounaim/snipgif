This policy explains how Snipgif and its partners use cookies and similar
browser storage. Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser.
Local storage works the same way but is never sent to a server automatically.

## What Snipgif stores itself

Snipgif's own code sets no cookies. It saves one value in your browser's local
storage:

- **`theme`** remembers whether you chose the light, dark, or system theme. It
  is saved only when you use the theme switch, stays on your device, and is
  never sent anywhere. Until then, the site follows your device setting.

Your videos and GIFs are never saved in cookies or storage. They stay in memory
and are cleared when you close the page.

## Cookies from Google

Snipgif uses, or will use, these Google services, which can set their own
cookies:

- **Google AdSense** uses cookies to show ads, including ads based on your
  interests, to limit how often you see an ad, and to measure ad performance.
- **Google Analytics 4** uses cookies to count visits and understand how the
  tools are used.
- **Google's consent management platform** uses a cookie to remember your
  consent choices.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are
asked for consent before advertising or analytics cookies that need consent are
used. Learn more about
[how Google uses cookies in advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
and manage personalized ads in [Google's My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

You can review or change your consent choices at any time with the **Privacy and
cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page.

## How to control cookies and storage

You can view, block, or delete cookies and local storage in your browser
settings, usually under Privacy or Site data. Blocking Google cookies does not
stop the converter from working. Clearing site data resets your theme choice.

For how Snipgif handles personal data, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
